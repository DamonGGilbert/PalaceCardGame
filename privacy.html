Shedhead – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Shedhead ("the game") is an independent card game made in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email us at shedheadgame@gmail.com.

In short: you don't need an account to play, and there are no ads. We don't sell your information. Your progress stays on your device. Online games and purchases use trusted services (Unity, Apple, Google) that process some information so those features work.

1. Information saved on your device

The game saves your settings, chosen name, coins, unlocked items, statistics, achievements and daily challenge progress on your device. It isn't sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Playing online with friends

When you play online, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay) to connect players. To do this, an anonymous player ID is created for your device, and the name you chose, your profile picture and cosmetic choices are shared with the other players in your game. Unity's servers process your device's IP address to connect the game; we don't store it. Players can only send each other preset reactions; there is no free-text chat. Same-Wi-Fi games connect devices directly on your local network.

3. Purchases

You can buy bags of coins. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and we never see your payment details. The store gives the game a receipt and transaction ID so it can give you what you bought.

4. No ads

The game shows no adverts and doesn't use advertising services, your device's advertising ID or your location. Unity, the engine the game is built with, may collect limited technical information (such as device model, performance and crash data) to run and improve its engine and services, as described in Unity's privacy policy.

5. Children

Shedhead is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the online features, contact us and we will delete any related data.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Play offline: no information leaves your device except what your app store handles.
  • Turn off quick reactions in the game's settings.
  • Ask for a copy of, or deletion of, data linked to your anonymous player ID: email shedheadgame@gmail.com with the subject "Data request". We respond within 30 days.
  • If you are in the UK or EU you also have rights under the GDPR, including to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
