Shedhead – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Shedhead ("the game") is made by [YOUR NAME / COMPANY] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

In short: you don't need an account to play. We don't sell your information. Most of what the game saves stays on your device. Online features, purchases and optional ads use trusted services (Unity, Apple, Google) that process some information so those features work.

1. Information saved on your device

The game saves your settings, chosen name, coins, unlocked items, statistics, achievements and daily challenge progress on your device. This stays on your device unless you turn on cloud backup (section 3). Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Playing online with friends

When you play online, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay) to connect players. To do this, an anonymous player ID is created for your device, and the name you chose, your profile picture and cosmetic choices are shared with the other players in your game. Unity's servers process your device's IP address to connect the game; we don't store it. Players can only send each other preset reactions; there is no free-text chat. Same-Wi-Fi games connect devices directly on your local network.

3. Cloud backup (optional)

If cloud save is available in your version of the game, your progress (coins, unlocks, statistics) is backed up with Unity Cloud Save, linked to an anonymous player ID. If you choose to sign in with a Unity account, your progress is linked to that account so you can restore it on another device. You can ask us to delete your cloud data at any time (section 8).

4. Purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. We receive a receipt and transaction ID so we can give you what you bought and restore it on a new device.

5. Ads (optional, rewarded only)

The game never shows forced ads. You can choose to watch an ad for bonus coins. Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners, which may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and how you interact with the ad, to show ads and measure them. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and EU), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the game's settings. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See Unity's privacy policy.

6. Notifications (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a notification on your device. It is created on your device; no notification data is sent to us.

7. Children

Shedhead is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the online features, contact us and we will delete any related data.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Play offline: no information leaves your device except what your app store handles.
  • Turn off reactions, reminders and (where offered) personalised ads in the game's settings.
  • Ask for a copy of, or deletion of, data linked to your player ID or Unity account: email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the subject "Data request". We respond within 30 days.
  • If you are in the UK or EU you also have rights under the GDPR, including to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
