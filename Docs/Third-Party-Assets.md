# Third-party assets in Shedhead

Everything not listed here is made for this game: it's either drawn in code or the Shedhead logo.

## Classic court card pictures
- **Files:** `Assets/Palace/Resources/Courts/court_{jack|queen|king}_{clubs|diamonds|hearts|spades}.png` (12 images)
- **Source:** Byron Knoll, "Vector Playing Cards" v1.3 (originally code.google.com/p/vector-playing-cards; blog post
  byronknoll.blogspot.com/2011/03/vector-playing-cards.html). Downloaded 26 Nov 2026 from
  https://opengameart.org/content/playing-cards-vector-png, where it's listed as **CC0** (public domain).
- **Licence:** released into the public domain by the author. The figures are traced from 19th-century court card
  designs, which are themselves long out of copyright. No attribution is required, but it's given in Credits anyway.
- **What we use:** only the 12 detailed court pictures (the `*2.png` versions), cropped to their picture frame.
  The pip cards, aces, corner labels and suit symbols are all drawn by the game. In particular, the set's
  Ace of Spades (by a different artist, Suzanne Tyson, with no clear licence) and its jokers are **not** used.
