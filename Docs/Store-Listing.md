## Full description (Google Play max 4000 / App Store max 4000)

Don't be the last one holding cards!

Shedhead is the classic shedding card game you played at school – the one with special cards, sneaky burns and that
one friend who always ends up with the whole pile. Get rid of your hand, then your face-up cards, then gamble on your
face-down cards. The last player left is the Shedhead.

**PLAY YOUR WAY**
• Single player against 1–9 computer players, from relaxed Easy to ruthless Hard
• Play with friends on the same Wi-Fi, or online anywhere with a join code
• 2 to 10 players at one table
• A guided first game teaches every special card as it comes up

**ALL THE CLASSIC RULES**
• 2 resets, 7 calls higher or lower, 8 skips, 10 burns the pile, Queen reverses
• Aces can be any value – or burn the pile
• Four of a kind in a row burns the pile
• Optional "Swap before play" mode for extra strategy

**MAKE IT YOURS**
• Hundreds of combinations: tables, decks, card backs, banners, name styles and fonts
• Premium range: Holographic, Galaxy, Gold Filigree, Dragon Scale and more
• 42 country flag tables, card backs and banners
• 10 burn effects and 5 win celebrations – your friends see them too
• Profile pictures that unlock as you level up

**KEEP COMING BACK**
• Levels, 27 achievements and lifetime stats
• Three new daily challenges every day
• Daily streak rewards
• Friendly quick reactions and emojis – no chat, no trolls

**FAIR AND FRIENDLY**
• Everything in the game can be earned by playing
• No forced ads – watch an ad for bonus coins only if you want to
• English, Polish and Spanish

Shuffle up. Burn the pile. Don't be the Shedhead.
